A playground for designers.

Create-o-matic is a free, open-source lab for type, colour, motion and texture. Set specimens, study palettes, stretch words and logos into loops, run anything through print and code effects, and pull it all together into a brand kit. It all exports: video, images or code. We hope it's useful.

Texture labAny image or video

A darkroom for anything.

Drop in a photo or a video at any size, or treat a loop straight from Motion, the Type tester, the Colour lab or the Brand bench. Stack risograph, halftone, photocopy, ASCII, paper, grunge, film, chroma split, holographic foil, gradient maps and your own scanned textures, in any order, each with its own blend mode and opacity. Drag across the picture to compare before and after. Export at full size, or reframe it for any format.

Type testerLive specimens

Every font, side by side.

Rows of your own words, each in its own font from 2,000+ open families, your Adobe Fonts project, your computer or your files. Size, weight, tracking and leading per row; stacks, grids and waterfalls; weight sweeps, tickers and typewriters. Vary deals you fonts you've never tried. Export a specimen sheet, or a loop.

Colour labPalettes, studied

See what your colours do to each other.

Lay your inks out as a printer's swatch card, as Albers' nested squares, as bands in proportion, as tints and shades, or set every one on every other and see which will read. Vary deals new palettes by harmony. Take the study away as an image or a loop, and the palette as CSS, JSON or an Adobe swatch file.

Brand benchYour brand, together

Drop in a logo. Leave with a kit.

An SVG keeps its colours; a PNG or JPEG loses its background. Take the palette from the logo, choose a face for each role, set the logo moving (or run it through ASCII and halftone), and see it all on one board. Then download the kit: design tokens for Figma and Style Dictionary, CSS, the logo, and a BRAND.md that Claude and other agents can design from. Or send the brand on to Motion, the Colour lab and the Type tester.

How it worksFour stages

Type in, loop out.

  1. Type and marks

    Write a word per line, or drop in an SVG logo. Choose from 2,000+ open fonts, your own Adobe Fonts kit or font files of your own.

  2. Grid and motion

    Cells breathe in waves that travel down the rows and across the columns, so letters swell, squash and swap. Or cut the frame by the golden section, or fill the shape of a logo.

  3. Keyframes and effects

    Animate any number or colour across the loop, as in After Effects. Add liquid, melt, halftone, photocopy, a moving gradient; fold it onto a cube, mirror it, or press it into a pin button.

  4. Export

    Rendered offline, frame by frame, at any size: every social format, 2.39:1 title cards, A3 posters at 300 dpi. Every loop closes perfectly.

For studiosAnd brands

A motion system you can hand to a team.

  • Lock a brand into presetsType, colour and marks saved as patches, so anyone can make loops that are on-brand by construction.
  • Every size at onceThe same patch at 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 and print. Change the format, keep the work.
  • Logos that moveDrop in an SVG mark and it stretches like type, or the type fills its silhouette.
  • Type over footageBring in a video, set the frame to 9:16, and put moving type over it, blended however you like.
  • Website heroesEmbed the live engine instead of shipping a heavy video. It even rests when it's off screen.
  • Title cards and posters2.39:1 scope frames for film titles; A4 and A3 at 300 dpi for print.
  • Campaigns and merchPut a loop on a pin button for a mock-up, or set a slogan into a golden-section poster.
  • Gradients on their ownLeave the words out and it's a gradient maker: mesh, linear or radial, looping, with grain.

Free, and yours to bend.

# run it locally (Node 22+)
git clone https://github.com/thisisnickdecker/create-o-matic
cd create-o-matic
npm run dev
