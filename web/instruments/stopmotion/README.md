# Stop motion

Stop-motion loops from your own photos and drawings. Add frames in order. Each drawing is held
on ones, twos or threes at its own rate, and played round or there and back. Boil shakes it a
little, as hand-registered work does. It all sits on a paper: a colour, film grain, riso dots or
your own scan, with a blend mode, so white paper in a photo drops away.

Open it at `/stopmotion/`.

## Making a loop

1. **Plan it.** In Planner, set the loop length and say what moves. It tells you how many
   drawings the loop takes at your rate and hold. **Copy the brief** gives you a prompt to paste
   into Claude or ChatGPT. It asks for every pose, what changes from the last, which frames are
   keys, how to make and shoot them so they register, and a checklist.
2. **Make the frames**: draw, print, cut, move, photograph. Keep the camera and the light fixed.
3. **Add them.** Click Add frames or drop them on the page. Chosen together, they're ordered by
   file name (frame-01, frame-02…), and Reverse flips the order.
4. **Lay them on paper**: Paper, Texture (or Your scan) and Blend. Multiply drops white paper
   out; Print in one ink turns every frame into one colour, like a single block or screen.

Until you add frames, nine sample block-printed headstones play, made by the app.

## Exports

- PNG, MP4, WebM, GIF and MOV in the Print shop. The video frame rate is the export's; your
  drawings keep their own rate and holds inside it.
- **Frames as a carousel (PNG)**: each drawing on its paper, one PNG each, numbered, to post as
  a carousel that swipes like the animation (Instagram takes up to 20).

## How it was checked

- Three presets were rendered at two moments and looked at. That caught the sample frames'
  off-white paper showing as a pale card under Multiply; they're pure white now.
- Planner: a 2-second loop at 12 a second, on twos, there and back, comes to 7 drawings (12
  beats: forward 7, back 5). The copied brief was read back from the clipboard.
- Frames as a carousel saved a zip of 9 PNGs. An MP4 of the loop (2.67 s) was made in Chrome.

## What it can't do yet

- Drag frames into a new order (Reverse and Clear only, for now).
- Onion skin, to line up the next drawing over the last.
- Video files as frames, or capture from a camera.
